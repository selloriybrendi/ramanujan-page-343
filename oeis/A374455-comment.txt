Proposed additions to A374455 (Expansion of o.g.f. 1/(1 - 5*x - 10*x^2 - 10*x^3 - 5*x^4 - x^5)):

%C A374455 a(n) = Tr(theta*alpha^(n+1))/10, where theta = 2^(1/5) and alpha = 1/(theta-1) = 1 + theta + ... + theta^4 (a Pisot unit). Consequently 10*a(n) is the integer nearest to 2^(1/5)*(2^(1/5)-1)^(-(n+1)) for n = 3 and all n >= 5; this is the case n=0 of Ramanujan's claim on page 343 of the Lost Notebook (see Andrews-Berndt, Part IV, Sect. 7.4). - _Otakhon U. Kenjaev_, Sep 27 2026
%F A374455 G.f.: 1/(2 - (1+x)^5). - _Otakhon U. Kenjaev_, Sep 27 2026
